# Lu changelog

Every update gets a new entry at the top. The top entry is Lu's version, and
when he starts up on a version he has not announced yet, he posts its notes in
the lu-testing-environment channel. Write the notes for the people in the
server, not for programmers. Second number up for an ordinary update (v1.1,
v1.2…); first number up only for a big change.

## v1.1 — 2026-09-18

- you can tell lu what you want him to be able to do: "lu suggest: let people vote on the music", "lu idea - a command that rolls dice", or "lu feature request: ...". he writes it down word for word and passes it on
- he will not discuss or read suggestions back, and he only says he noted one if he really did

## v1.0 — 2026-09-17

- lu replies to what you actually said instead of giving a speech, and keeps it short
- lu can rename people: "lu rename @someone to potato", "lu give @someone a new name" or "lu reset someone's name" (you need the manage nicknames permission)
- lu posts here whenever he is updated, with the version and what changed
